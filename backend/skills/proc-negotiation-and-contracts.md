## When to use
Preparing or running a supplier negotiation; drafting or reviewing the commercial schedule of a supply agreement or PO. Liability, indemnity, IP and governing-law wording goes to legal review.

## Preparation
- Per issue: opening, target and reservation (walk-away) point.
- BATNA: what you do with no deal (another supplier, insource, delay). Price it on the same scope, terms and risk as the offer. Estimate the supplier's BATNA too.
- ZOPA: overlap between the buyer's maximum and the seller's minimum. No ZOPA: improve the BATNA, change scope, or walk away.
- Fact base: should-cost, market indices, supplier cost structure and margins, your share of their revenue, switching cost.
- Issue map: priorities and trades (cheap for you, valuable to them).

## Running it
- Anchor with a reasoned first offer; negotiate packages, not single issues; table multiple equivalent simultaneous offers to reveal preferences.
- Concession plan: shrinking steps, each traded ("if you ..., then we ..."), none unilateral; log every move.
- Confirm agreements in writing after each round; nothing is agreed until everything is agreed. One lead voice, an analyst, a note-taker.

## Key commercial terms
- Price basis, volume tiers, rebates; payment terms. Cost of skipping an early-payment discount = d/(1 - d) x 365/(N - t), d discount, t discount days, N net days: 2/10 net 30 = 2/98 x 365/20 = 37.2% a year simple, (1 + 2/98)^(365/20) - 1 = 44.6% compounded. Take it when that beats the cost of capital or short-term borrowing rate.
- Delivery: Incoterms rule and place, lead times, MOQs, forecast vs firm horizon, allocation in shortage.
- Quality: specification, acceptance, ppm targets, warranty.
- SLAs and KPIs: OTIF, defect ppm, lead time, response and resolution times, each with formula, data source, period and owner; service credits (usually capped at a share of monthly charges) kept separate from damages; termination for repeated failure.
- Change control, business continuity, audit rights, code of conduct and ESG duties, exit assistance.

## Price adjustment clauses
Adjusted price = base price x (current index / base-period index). For a composite, weight component indices (materials, energy, labour) so weights sum to 1.0. Name the exact series title and code, base month (not a hard-coded index value), interval and calculation date; agree whether first-published or final values apply (BLS PPI values can be revised for up to 4 months); use not seasonally adjusted series; work in percent change, not index points; add a successor or fallback index, caps or floors and a minimum trigger. Index to cost drivers, never to the product's own price.

## Incoterms 2020 in POs
- Write rule + named place + edition, e.g. "FCA Seller's warehouse, Haiphong, Incoterms 2020".
- Any mode: EXW, FCA, CPT, CIP, DAP, DPU, DDP. Sea and inland waterway only: FAS, FOB, CFR, CIF. Containers: FCA, CPT or CIP, because risk should pass at handover to the carrier, not on board.
- CIP requires Institute Cargo Clauses (A) cover; CIF defaults to (C). DPU means the seller unloads.
- Incoterms set delivery point, risk, costs and clearance; they do not transfer title, fix price or payment, or give breach remedies.

## Output
Negotiation plan (issues, targets, BATNA, concessions), term sheet, KPI schedule with formulas, price adjustment clause, outcome log.

## Pitfalls
No priced BATNA; conceding without a trade; KPIs without measurement rules; FOB for containers; omitting the Incoterms edition.
