## When to use
Comparing quotes, budgeting freight, auditing invoices, disputing demurrage or detention, running a tender or a carrier business review.

## Ocean rate build-up
All-in = base freight + surcharges + origin and destination charges.
| Code | Meaning | Check |
|---|---|---|
| BAF, LSS | bunker adjustment, low-sulphur fuel | index, period, formula |
| CAF | currency adjustment | percent of base |
| THC (OTHC, DTHC) | terminal handling | pass-through or marked up |
| GRI | general rate increase | US tariff increases need 30 days' notice |
| PSS | peak season surcharge | start and end dates |
| ETS, FuelEU | EU emissions cost recovery | per-unit method; ETS covers 100 percent intra-EU and 50 percent of extra-EU voyage emissions |
| War risk, contingency | e.g. Red Sea diversion | expiry trigger |
Other accessorials: documentation and B/L fees, ISPS, seal, telex release, chassis, pre-pull, waiting time, storage, reefer plug-in, carrier vs merchant haulage.

## Demurrage and detention
- Demurrage: full or empty box held in the terminal beyond free time. Detention (per diem): carrier equipment kept outside beyond free time. Combined clocks are common.
- Cost = chargeable days x daily rate per tier. Negotiate free days in the contract (e.g. 14 combined at destination) instead of event waivers.
- US billing rule (46 CFR part 541, in force 28 May 2024): invoice within 30 days of the last charge day, showing B/L and container numbers, free time and its dates, days and rates charged, the tariff or contract basis, dispute contact and a compliance certification; billed party gets at least 30 days to dispute and the biller aims to resolve within 30. A late invoice or one missing required content need not be paid. Section 541.4 (who may be billed) was set aside by the D.C. Circuit in 2025 and removed.
- Fees must incentivise cargo fluidity (46 CFR 545.5): in 2026 the D.C. Circuit upheld the FMC (Evergreen v. FMC) against detention charged while a three-day port closure blocked returns.
- Prevent: pre-clear, book trucks before ETA, track last free day, return empties fast.

## Tender method
1. Baseline 12 months by lane, equipment, month; forecast with a range.
2. RFQ: lanes, volumes, equipment, Incoterms, service needs (transit, free time, reefer), payment terms, contract type (fixed, index-linked to SCFI, Drewry WCI or Xeneta, or hybrid), validity.
3. Normalise bids to all-in per FEU including surcharges and expected D&D.
4. Score: cost 50-60 percent, reliability and capacity 20-30, service, IT and emissions data 10-20. Award primary and backup per lane; cap single-carrier share on critical lanes.
5. Contract: minimum quantity commitment, space guarantee, peak allocation, surcharge rules, free time, dispute terms.

## KPIs and formulas
- Schedule reliability (global 49.9 percent in August 2026, late vessels averaging 6.81 days), booking acceptance, rollover rate, transit variance, OTIF, invoice accuracy, claims ratio, D&D per container, CO2e per TEU-km (ISO 14083:2023).
- Landed cost = goods + freight + insurance + duty + non-recoverable taxes + brokerage + D&D + inventory carrying cost (transit days x daily goods value x annual cost of capital / 365).
- Cost per unit = all-in per container / units per container; compare at the same fill rate.

## Output
Bid table at all-in per FEU (base, each surcharge, local charges, expected D&D, transit, reliability), then the award or dispute position with the clause or rule relied on.

## Pitfalls
- Comparing base rates only; surcharges can exceed base freight.
- Fixed rates in a falling market or pure index in a rising one without a band.
- Paying D&D without checking free time, closures and billing rules.
