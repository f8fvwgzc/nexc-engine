## When to use
Bringing a new product, feature, market or segment to customers; choosing how buyers will discover, try, buy and expand; setting or changing prices; planning a launch.

## Motion
| Motion | Fits when | Lead metrics |
|---|---|---|
| Product-led | low price point, fast time to value, a single user can adopt, collaboration spreads it | activation rate, time to value, free-to-paid rate, product-qualified leads (PQLs), net revenue retention |
| Sales-led | high ACV, buying committee, security or integration review, services needed | pipeline coverage (rule of thumb 3-4x quota), win rate, cycle length, ACV, CAC payback |
| Hybrid (product-led sales) | self-serve entry, enterprise upside | PQL-to-opportunity rate, expansion revenue |
Define the ideal customer profile (firmographics, trigger events, tech stack, pain) and the buying committee before picking a motion. Net revenue retention = (starting ARR + expansion - contraction - churn) / starting ARR, for the same cohort over 12 months.

## Pricing
- Value-based: economic value to customer = price of next-best alternative + net differentiation value. Price sits between the cost floor and that value; capture part of the differentiation.
- Value metric: per seat, per usage unit or per outcome; it should grow as customer value grows.
- Research: Van Westendorp (too cheap, cheap, expensive, too expensive; acceptable range between marginal cheapness and marginal expensiveness, optimal and indifference points), Gabor-Granger (buy probability at set prices, revenue-maximising point), conjoint (willingness to pay by feature).
- Packaging: good-better-best with clear fences (features, limits, support, SLA). Freemium = permanent limited free tier; free trial = full access for a period; reverse trial = full trial that drops to free.
- Price change breakeven: required volume change = -dP / (CM + dP), with dP the price change and CM the contribution margin, both as fractions of price. A 10 % cut at 40 % margin needs +33 % units.

## Channels
Direct field or inside sales, self-serve web, cloud or app marketplaces, resellers and VARs, distributors, OEM, referral and affiliate. Match cost to serve to ACV and to how buyers prefer to buy. Set conflict rules: deal registration, price floors, territories, partner margins.

## Launch tiers
- Tier 1 (new product, market or pricing): full campaign, PR and analyst briefings, sales enablement; 8-12 weeks lead.
- Tier 2 (significant feature): blog, email, webinar, enablement; 4-6 weeks.
- Tier 3 (minor): release notes, in-app message; 1-2 weeks.
Tier by revenue at stake, customers affected, competitive significance and behaviour change required.

## Output
GTM plan: objective and KPIs with targets; ICP and segments; positioning and messaging; pricing and packaging with research basis; channel plan; enablement kit (battlecard, demo, FAQ, objection handling); readiness gates (product GA, docs, support trained, billing, legal and privacy review); T-minus timeline with RACI; 30/60/90-day measurement; rollback criteria.

## Pitfalls
- Choosing PLG for a product that needs implementation work.
- Cost-plus pricing that ignores customer value; too many tiers.
- Launch dates without readiness gates; no owner per KPI.
- Partners recruited without margin, enablement or conflict rules.
