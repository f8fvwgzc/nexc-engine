## When to use
Conduct questions in investment work: recommendations, trades, client messages, performance claims, gifts, conflicts, possible MNPI. Benchmark: Code and Standards revised 2023, effective 1 January 2024 (Standards of Practice Handbook, 12th edition).

## Standards map
| Standard | Test to apply |
|---|---|
| I(A) Knowledge of the Law | Follow the stricter of law and Code; dissociate from violations |
| I(B) Independence and Objectivity | Refuse gifts or pressure that could sway judgement; disclose client gifts to the employer |
| I(C) Misrepresentation | No false claims, guaranteed returns on risky assets or plagiarism |
| I(D) Misconduct | No dishonesty, fraud or deceit |
| I(E) Competence (new 2024) | Act with and keep the competence the role needs |
| II(A) MNPI | Material + nonpublic: do not trade, change a rating or cause others to trade; a mosaic of public and non-material facts is fine |
| II(B) Market Manipulation | No misleading information or trades distorting price or volume |
| III(A) Loyalty, Prudence, Care | Client first, then employer, then self; identify the client |
| III(B) Fair Dealing | Tell all clients of changes fairly; pro-rata block and IPO allocation |
| III(C) Suitability | Know the client (IPS); judge fit at portfolio level; funds follow the mandate |
| III(D) Performance Presentation | Fair, accurate, complete; no cherry-picked periods or accounts |
| III(E) Confidentiality | Client data private unless law requires or client consents |
| IV(A)-(C) Employer duties | No soliciting before leaving; written consent for outside pay; supervisors keep adequate controls |
| V(A) Diligence and Reasonable Basis | Research thoroughly; test third-party and model inputs |
| V(B) Communication | Disclose process, limits, changes, nature of services and costs (2024); fact vs opinion |
| V(C) Record Retention | Keep support for recommendations (7 years if no rule applies) |
| VI(A) Avoid or Disclose Conflicts | Avoid; else disclose prominently in plain language |
| VI(B) Priority of Transactions | Client and employer trades before personal ones |
| VI(C) Referral Fees | Disclose referral compensation |
| VII(A)-(B) CFA Program and designation | No exam breaches; never imply a partial designation ("CFA Level II"); no claim the charter guarantees results |

## Method
1. List actors, the client and duties owed (client, employer, market).
2. Pin trigger facts: source and timing of information, materiality, beneficiary.
3. Map facts to standards (stricter of law and Code); decide violation, none or remediate, citing the Standard.
4. Prescribe action (stop, disclose, get consent, escalate, document) and controls: firewalls, restricted and watch lists, pre-clearance, blackout periods, allocation policy.
5. MNPI found: no trades or rating changes on the issuer, tell compliance, ask the issuer to publish it if possible.

## GIPS (2020 edition, effective 1 January 2020)
- Firm-wide compliance (no partial claims); every actual fee-paying discretionary segregated account sits in at least one composite with criteria set in advance.
- Time-weighted by default; money-weighted only if the firm controls external cash flows and the portfolio is closed-end, fixed-life, fixed-commitment or mainly illiquid.
- Give GIPS Reports to prospects; correct material errors; verification is recommended, not required.

## Output
Facts with sources; each Standard engaged with verdict and reason; actions and controls; facts that would change the answer.

## Pitfalls
- Believing that telling a client or the firm makes MNPI usable: only public release does.
- Claiming any designation for yourself or the agent.
- Treating legal-but-unethical as fine.
- Giving binding regulatory advice: name the jurisdiction, refer to compliance or counsel.
