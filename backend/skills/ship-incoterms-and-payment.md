## When to use
Drafting or reviewing a quote, sales contract, PO or payment term; deciding who books freight, insures, clears customs or bears a transit loss. Incoterms 2020 (in force 1 January 2020) is the current ICC edition as of 2026; a contract may still name 2010, so apply the edition it names.

## The eleven rules
Write `<rule> <precise named place> Incoterms 2020`; "FOB China" is a defect.

| Rule | Risk passes to buyer | Seller pays main carriage | Import clearance |
|---|---|---|---|
| EXW | at buyer's disposal at seller's place, not loaded | no | buyer (buyer exports too) |
| FCA | handed to buyer's carrier (loaded, if at seller's premises) | no | buyer |
| CPT, CIP | handed to first carrier | yes | buyer |
| DAP | ready for unloading at destination | yes | buyer |
| DPU | unloaded at destination | yes | buyer |
| DDP | ready for unloading, duties paid | yes | seller |
| FAS | alongside ship, loading port | no | buyer |
| FOB | on board, loading port | no | buyer |
| CFR, CIF | on board, loading port | yes | buyer |

- FAS, FOB, CFR, CIF are for sea and inland waterway only. Containers handed over at a terminal or CFS take FCA, CPT or CIP; otherwise the seller keeps risk until loading.
- C-rules split two points: risk passes at shipment, cost runs to destination.
- Seller clears export except under EXW; buyer clears import except under DDP. Costs sit in A9/B9, security duties in A4/A7.
- FCA A6/B6 option: buyer instructs its carrier to issue an on-board B/L to the seller, for LCs that require one.

## Insurance
- CIF minimum: Institute Cargo Clauses (C). CIP minimum: ICC (A), unless agreed otherwise.
- Sum insured at least contract price plus 10 percent, in contract currency, from delivery point to destination.
- Other rules impose no insurance duty: tell the party at risk to insure.

## Payment (seller risk falls along this line)
Open account, documentary collection, letter of credit, cash in advance.
- Documentary collection (ICC URC 522): D/P (documents against payment) or D/A (against acceptance of a draft); banks give no payment guarantee.
- Letter of credit (UCP 600, 39 articles, effective 1 July 2007; eUCP 2.1 for electronic records; ISBP for document checking): the bank's undertaking is separate from the sale (Art. 4-5) and banks deal in documents only.
- UCP 600 rules to apply: up to five banking days to examine (Art. 14b); present within 21 calendar days after shipment and before expiry (Art. 14c); refuse once, listing every discrepancy (Art. 16); "about" means plus or minus 10 percent (Art. 30); insurance at least 110 percent of CIF or CIP value (Art. 28); confirmation adds a second bank's undertaking.

## Checklist
- Rule matches mode and handover point; place precise; edition named.
- Incoterm, LC documents, B/L type and insurance agree with each other.
- Latest shipment date, presentation period and expiry leave time to get documents.
- Customs value basis checked (EU and Japan add freight to the border; US values FOB).

## Output
- Recommended term written in full, e.g. `FCA Shenzhen CFS, Incoterms 2020`, with the reason in one line.
- Responsibility matrix: export clearance, pre-carriage, loading, main carriage, insurance, unloading, import clearance, duties and VAT, each marked seller or buyer, plus the exact risk-transfer point.
- Payment term with the document list, latest shipment date, presentation period and expiry; open questions for the parties.

## Pitfalls
- FOB or CIF for containers; DDP when the seller cannot register for import VAT; EXW when the buyer cannot act as exporter.
- Assuming Incoterms move title or fix price; they do neither.
- LCs requiring documents the seller cannot control, such as a buyer-signed inspection certificate.
