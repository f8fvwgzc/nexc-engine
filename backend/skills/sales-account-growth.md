## When to use
Planning key accounts, running customer success and business reviews, diagnosing churn risk, sizing expansion and reporting retention.

## Retention metrics (one starting cohort, usually 12 months)
- GRR = (starting ARR - contraction - churn) / starting ARR; it can never exceed 100%.
- NRR (NDR) = (starting ARR + expansion - contraction - churn) / starting ARR; new logos are excluded.
- Logo churn = customers lost / customers at start.
- Example: start 10.0M, expansion 1.5M, contraction 0.4M, churn 0.6M gives GRR 90% and NRR 105%.
- Reference points (segment-dependent, verify locally): NRR above 100% means the base grows on its own; private B2B SaaS medians near 101% NRR and 90% GRR (KBCM/Sapphire 2024 survey, cited by Gainsight); enterprise leaders exceed 120%. NRR more than 30 points above GRR suggests a few expanding accounts are hiding broad churn.
- Simple customer lifetime value = annual gross margin per customer / annual churn rate.

## Account plan
Overview and strategy; whitespace map (products x business units: owned, in progress, open, competitor); relationship map (power, stance, coverage; multi-thread to three or more senior contacts); the customer's goals and value delivered so far; health; risks; expansion opportunities with value and timing; action plan with owners and dates; executive sponsor pairing.

## Customer health
Weight leading indicators and band accounts red, amber or green: usage against entitlement, depth of feature adoption, support ticket trend and severity, sentiment (NPS, CSAT, verbatims), engagement (cadence calls, follow-through on business reviews), an active executive sponsor, outcomes achieved, commercial signals (late payment, renewal date near). Back-test the weights against past churn and rework the model when churn surprises you.

## Churn signals and saves
Usage down 20-30% in a quarter, champion or sponsor leaves, merger or new leadership, budget cuts, unresolved P1 escalations, low NPS, procurement asking about termination terms, competitor evaluation, no renewal talk 90 days out. A save plan names the root cause, executive outreach, remediation steps and dates.

## QBR or EBR
Pre-read sent ahead. Agenda: their objectives; value delivered against agreed metrics, quantified; usage and adoption; support summary; roadmap items relevant to their goals; recommendations; next-period goals and mutual actions. Keep about 70% on them and 30% on you, and invite the economic buyer.

## Expansion plays
Upsell (tier, seats, usage), cross-sell (adjacent product), land and expand into new business units, renewal uplift tied to proven value, partner-led. Trigger from value proof, usage nearing limits or new initiatives. Fix a red account before trying to expand it.

## Output
Account plan, health table, retention report (GRR, NRR, logo churn by segment and cohort), QBR outline, save plan.

## Pitfalls
Mixing ARR with bookings; counting new logos in NRR; QBRs that become product demos; health scores nobody back-tests; single-threaded relationships.
