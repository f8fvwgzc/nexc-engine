## When to use
Tax provisions, deferred tax, indirect tax, transfer pricing and Pillar Two work. Tax is always local: name the jurisdiction, tax year and law version, state assumptions, give general information only and refer binding positions and filings to a licensed tax adviser.

## Income tax and deferred tax
- Current tax = taxable profit x statutory rate; taxable profit = accounting profit +/- permanent differences +/- movements in temporary differences.
- Deferred tax = (carrying amount - tax base) x rate expected at reversal (IAS 12: enacted or substantively enacted; ASC 740: enacted).
- Deferred tax assets: IAS 12 recognises to the extent future taxable profit is probable; ASC 740 recognises all, then a valuation allowance unless realisation is more likely than not. Evidence: reversing DTLs, forecasts, tax planning, loss history.
- Uncertain positions: IFRIC 23 uses most likely amount or expected value, assuming full examination; ASC 740-10 recognises if more likely than not, measured at the largest amount over 50 percent likely.
- ETR reconciliation: statutory rate, permanent items, foreign rate differences, credits, allowance changes, prior-year true-ups, top-up tax. US PBEs apply the ASU 2023-09 categories and taxes paid by jurisdiction. IAS 12 forbids deferred tax on Pillar Two top-up tax; disclose current top-up tax separately.

## VAT, GST and consumption tax
- Net payable = output tax - recoverable input tax; recovery needs a valid invoice and taxable use; exempt supplies restrict recovery (apportion).
- Decide per supply: taxable, zero-rated or exempt; place of supply; B2B reverse charge vs B2C; registration threshold; invoice content; filing period.
- Japan: 10 percent standard, 8 percent reduced (food and drink except alcohol and dining out; subscribed newspapers, 2+ issues a week). Since 1 Oct 2023 input credit needs a qualified invoice from a registered issuer (T plus 13 digits) with amounts and tax by rate. Purchases from unregistered suppliers: 80 percent credit to 30 Sep 2026; FY2026 reform (NTA guidance, Apr 2026): 70 percent Oct 2026-Sep 2028, 50 to Sep 2030, 30 to Sep 2031, then nil; no credit on purchases from one unregistered supplier above JPY 100m a year (was 1bn) for periods starting from 1 Oct 2026.

## Transfer pricing
- Arm's length principle (OECD Model Art. 9; OECD TP Guidelines 2022). Delineate the transaction: contract terms, functions, assets and risks, characteristics, economic circumstances, business strategy.
- Methods: CUP, resale price, cost plus, TNMM, profit split; pick the most appropriate, test with comparables (interquartile range).
- Documentation: master file, local file, CbC report (group revenue EUR 750m+).

## Pillar Two (GloBE)
- Scope: consolidated revenue EUR 750m+ in at least 2 of the 4 preceding years.
- Per jurisdiction: ETR = adjusted covered taxes / GloBE income; top-up % = 15% - ETR; top-up tax = top-up % x (GloBE income - substance-based income exclusion) + additional top-up - QDMTT.
- Order: QDMTT locally, IIR at the parent, UTPR as backstop; file the GloBE Information Return.
- Side-by-side package (5 Jan 2026): groups whose UPE is in a qualified SbS jurisdiction (US first; see the OECD central record) are relieved of IIR and UTPR for fiscal years from 1 Jan 2026; QDMTTs still apply. Also a permanent simplified ETR safe harbour (from 2027, some 2026), a substance-based tax incentive safe harbour, and the transitional CbCR safe harbour extended one year.

## Output
Jurisdiction and period, facts, rules cited, computation table, assumptions, risks, open points, adviser referral.

## Pitfalls
Using today's rate, not the rate enacted for the reversal year; netting DTAs across jurisdictions; ignoring invoice requirements; applying OECD text where local law differs.
