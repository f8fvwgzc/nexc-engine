## When to use
Setting up or reviewing a content programme: strategy, pillars, calendar, distribution, workflow, governance and reporting.

## Method
1. Goals: tie content to a business objective and one metric per funnel stage (awareness: reach, share of search = your brand searches / all brand searches in the category; consideration: engaged sessions, subscribers; conversion: leads, pipeline influenced; retention: activation, support deflection). Targets are SMART and start from a baseline.
2. Audience: 2-4 personas or jobs to be done, each with its questions by buying stage, drawn from sales calls, search data, tickets and reviews.
3. Content audit: inventory every asset (URL, title, type, topic, stage, traffic, conversions, links, last updated, owner) and decide keep, update, merge, redirect or retire. Assets with no traffic, links or conversions for 12 months are merge or retire candidates.
4. Pillars: 3-5 themes where audience need, business expertise and search or social demand overlap; each gets a pillar asset and supporting pieces linked to it.
5. Mix: hero (large, periodic), hub (regular series), help (evergreen answers); or about 70/20/10 proven, new and experimental. Match format to stage: guides and explainers early, comparisons and case studies mid, calculators, demos and pricing pages late.
6. Editorial calendar fields: publish date, title, pillar, persona, stage, format, primary keyword, owner, reviewer, status (idea > brief > draft > edit > SME/legal review > scheduled > published > refresh due), channels, CTA. Plan a cadence you can sustain and batch production.
7. Distribution, create once and publish everywhere: owned (site, email, community), earned (PR, partners, search), shared (social), paid (boost proven winners). Repurpose each pillar into native formats per channel (short posts, carousel, email, clips, slides) instead of pasting one text everywhere.
8. Governance: style guide and voice, RACI for strategist, editor, SME, legal and publisher, approval SLAs, a review date on every asset (regulated or YMYL topics more often), accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA, an AI-use policy with human fact-checking, archive and redirect rules.
9. Measurement:
   - GA4 engagement rate = engaged sessions / sessions; an engaged session lasts over 10 s, has a key event or has 2+ page or screen views; bounce rate = 1 - engagement rate.
   - Search Console clicks, impressions, CTR and average position per page or cluster.
   - Content-assisted conversions and pipeline (state the attribution model), email click rate, cost per asset and per lead.
   Report monthly; revisit strategy quarterly.

## Checklist
- Goals with baselines and one KPI per stage
- Audit done with a decision per asset
- Pillars approved; calendar has owners and dates
- Distribution plan per asset; review dates and governance set

## Output
Strategy document: summary, objectives and KPIs table, audiences, audit findings, pillars, content mix, calendar table, distribution matrix (asset x channel), governance RACI, measurement plan, 90-day plan, risks.

## Pitfalls
Volume over value; no owner for refreshes; pageviews as the only metric; publishing without distribution; last-click attribution that hides content's influence; calendars no team can staff.
