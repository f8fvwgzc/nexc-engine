## When to use
Recognition, measurement, presentation or disclosure under IFRS or US GAAP (ASC); technical memos; dual reporting.

## Method
1. Fix framework and reporting date; name the standard and paragraph or ASC topic; check effective date and transition.
2. Apply the model below to the facts. Memo: issue, facts, paragraphs cited, analysis, conclusion, entries, disclosures, judgements with numeric effect.

**Revenue (IFRS 15 / ASC 606), five steps:** (1) enforceable contract, commercial substance, collection probable; (2) performance obligations = distinct goods or services (capable of being distinct, and distinct in context); (3) transaction price, with variable consideration constrained so a significant reversal is highly probable (IFRS) / probable (ASC) not to occur; adjust for financing and amounts payable to the customer; (4) allocate on relative stand-alone selling prices; (5) recognise over time if the customer consumes as you perform, controls the asset as built, or no alternative use + enforceable right to payment; else when control passes.

**Leases:** IFRS 16 lessees book a right-of-use asset and a liability at the PV of payments (implicit rate, else IBR); expense is front-loaded (depreciation + interest); exemptions: short-term (12 months or less, no purchase option) and low-value. ASC 842 classifies finance vs operating (ownership transfer, purchase option reasonably certain, major part of economic life, PV substantially all of fair value, specialised asset); operating: straight-line single lease cost.

**Financial instruments (IFRS 9):** debt assets at amortised cost (hold to collect + SPPI), FVOCI (collect and sell + SPPI) or FVTPL; equities FVTPL or FVOCI election (no recycling); three-stage ECL. May 2024 amendments (from 1 Jan 2026): ESG-linked features in SPPI; optional early derecognition of liabilities paid electronically. US GAAP CECL (ASC 326): lifetime losses from day one.

**Consolidation:** IFRS 10 control = power + variable returns + ability to use power to affect them. ASC 810 applies the VIE model (primary beneficiary) before the voting-interest model. Goodwill = consideration + NCI + prior interest - fair value of net identifiable assets; NCI at fair value or proportionate share (IFRS), fair value (US GAAP).

**Impairment:** IAS 36 recoverable amount = higher of FV less costs of disposal and value in use, by CGU; goodwill tested yearly; reversals except for goodwill. US GAAP goodwill loss = reporting-unit carrying amount - fair value, capped at goodwill; ASC 360 long-lived assets: undiscounted recoverability test first; no reversals.

**IFRS 18** (replaces IAS 1; annual periods from 1 Jan 2027, retrospective): categories operating, investing, financing, income taxes, discontinued; subtotals operating profit and profit before financing and income taxes; MPMs in one note reconciled to the nearest IFRS subtotal incl. tax and NCI effects; by-function presenters disclose depreciation, amortisation, employee benefits, impairment and inventory write-downs; IAS 7 indirect method starts at operating profit; the interest and dividend classification choice goes. US PBEs: ASU 2023-09 tax disclosures (years beginning after 15 Dec 2024); ASU 2024-03 expense disaggregation (years beginning after 15 Dec 2026).

## Key differences
IFRS bans LIFO; IAS 38 capitalises qualifying development costs, ASC 730 expenses them; IFRS permits PPE revaluation and write-down reversals; IFRS provisions use more-likely-than-not and the midpoint of a range, US GAAP a higher bar and the low end.

## Pitfalls
Mixing the frameworks' thresholds; missing embedded leases; treating IFRS 18 as a measurement change; citing IAS 17, or IAS 39 outside the hedge accounting choice; citing IAS 1 for 2027+ periods or IFRS 18 before adoption.
