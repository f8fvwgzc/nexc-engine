## When to use
Tiering suppliers, judging financial health, human-rights and environmental due diligence (CSDDD, LkSG, UFLPA), audits, key-supplier management.

## Risk tiers
Score criticality (revenue at stake, substitutability, switching time), spend and inherent risk (country, sector, financial, cyber, ESG).
- Critical: annual deep review, BCP evidence, audit, executive sponsor.
- High: annual questionnaire plus desk research. Medium/low: onboarding screen plus automated monitoring.
- Re-tier on triggers: ownership change, quality failure, adverse media, sanctions hit.

## Financial health signals
- UK Government EFS ratios: turnover ratio (supplier revenue / expected annual contract value), operating margin, free cash flow / net debt (capital-intensive) or net debt / EBITDA, (net debt + net pension deficit) / EBITDA, interest cover (EBIT / interest paid). Credit scores corroborate; they never decide alone.
- Altman Z (listed manufacturers): 1.2 X1 + 1.4 X2 + 3.3 X3 + 0.6 X4 + 1.0 X5 (working capital, retained earnings, EBIT and sales over total assets; equity market value over liabilities); below 1.81 distress, above 2.99 safe.
- Soft signals: early-payment requests, late payment of sub-suppliers, going-concern notes, CFO or auditor change, covenant pressure, refinancing due in 12-18 months.
- Mitigate: parent guarantee, bonds, step-in rights, escrow, second source, buffer stock, financial distress clauses.

## Human-rights and environmental due diligence
OECD cycle: embed policy; identify and assess impacts; cease, prevent, mitigate; track; communicate; remediate. Prioritise by severity (scale, scope, irremediability) and likelihood.
- EU CSDDD (Directive (EU) 2024/1760 as amended by Directive (EU) 2026/470): EU firms with 5,000+ staff and EUR 1.5 bn worldwide turnover, non-EU firms with EUR 1.5 bn EU turnover; transposition by 26 July 2028, application from 26 July 2029; focus on likely and severe impacts; fines up to 3% of worldwide turnover.
- German LkSG (from 2023 for 3,000+ and from 2024 for 1,000+ employees in Germany): risk management, annual and ad hoc risk analysis, policy statement, prevention and remedy for own operations and direct suppliers, indirect suppliers on substantiated knowledge, complaints procedure, documentation. A Cabinet bill of 3 September 2025 removes the reporting duty; BAFA has stopped reviewing reports. Check enactment and sanction changes locally.
- US UFLPA (presumption since 21 June 2022): goods made wholly or partly in Xinjiang or by a firm on the UFLPA Entity List are presumed made with forced labour and denied entry; to rebut, the importer must follow CBP guidance, answer every CBP request fully and prove by clear and convincing evidence that no forced labour was used. High-priority sectors: apparel, cotton, silica-based products incl. polysilicon, tomatoes, aluminium, PVC, seafood; added 2025: caustic soda, copper, lithium, red dates, steel. Evidence: trace to raw material with POs, invoices, production and transport records.

## Audits and SRM
Social audits (SMETA, amfori BSCI, RBA, SA8000) are snapshots: add worker voice, grievance data and unannounced visits; close corrective actions with dates. SRM: segment, joint scorecards (quality, delivery, cost, ESG, innovation), quarterly reviews, development plans, escalation.

## Output
Supplier risk register (tier, scores, signals, mitigation, owner, review date), due-diligence file per high-risk chain, corrective-action tracker, SRM scorecards.

## Pitfalls
Tiering by spend alone; treating a clean audit as proof; abrupt exit that harms workers; quoting dates without checking national law. Binding legal positions need qualified counsel in the jurisdiction.
