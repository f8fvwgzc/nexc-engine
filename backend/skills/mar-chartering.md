## When to use
Fixing a ship, reviewing a recap or charter party, settling a laytime statement, off-hire or performance claim. Outcomes turn on wording and governing law (often English law with LMAA arbitration, or New York or Singapore): name it and refer binding positions to maritime counsel or the P&I club.

## Charter types
| Type | Owner pays | Charterer pays | Forms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voyage | ship, crew, bunkers, port costs (cargo handling per FIO terms) | freight per tonne or lump sum, demurrage | GENCON 2022 or GENCON 94; ASBATANKVOY for tankers |
| Time | capital and operating costs | daily hire, bunkers, port costs, canal dues | NYPE 2015 (BIMCO, ASBA, SMF), NYPE 93; SHELLTIME 4 |
| Bareboat | capital only | crew, opex, insurance, full control | BARECON 2017 |

## Laytime and demurrage
1. Laytime allowed = cargo quantity / agreed rate (50,000 t at 10,000 t per day = 5 days) or fixed hours (tanker forms commonly 72 h plus 6 h after NOR).
2. Start: arrived ship (at the berth under a berth charter; in port and at charterers' disposal under a port charter), ready in all respects, valid NOR tendered as the clause requires, then any turn time. WIBON, WIPON, WCCON and WIFPON shift waiting risk to the charterer.
3. Exceptions follow the words: SHINC counts Sundays and holidays, SHEX excludes them; weather working days exclude bad-weather periods; also shifting, strikes, ship's gear breakdown.
4. Once on demurrage, always on demurrage: laytime exceptions stop applying unless the demurrage clause repeats them.
5. Demurrage = (time used - laytime allowed) x daily rate, pro rata. Despatch, if agreed, is often half the demurrage rate, on all time saved or working time saved.
6. Reversible or averaged laytime pools loading and discharge.
Apply BIMCO Laytime Definitions for Charter Parties 2013 where incorporated. A statement lists each period: from, to, counted, excepted and the reason.

## Time charter money
- Hire is paid in advance, often per 15 days; withdrawal for late payment usually needs an anti-technicality notice first.
- Off-hire: net time lost from the listed causes (breakdown, crew or stores deficiency, drydock, detention for owners' matters). Deduction = hours lost / 24 x daily hire, plus bunkers burnt in that period.
- Performance: speed and consumption warranted "about" (English practice allows roughly 0.5 knot and 5 percent) in defined good weather, e.g. up to Beaufort 4 and Douglas sea state 3.

## Clauses to check
Laycan and cancelling; safe port and safe berth; trading limits; sanctions, war risks (CONWARTIME, VOYWAR), piracy; emissions clauses for time charters (ETS allowances, CII operations, FuelEU Maritime) and the BIMCO Biofuel Clause for Time Charter Parties 2026; ETS clauses for BARECON and ship sale forms; Both-to-Blame Collision, New Jason, general average (York-Antwerp Rules 2016); paramount clause (Hague-Visby); Inter-Club NYPE Agreement for cargo claims; law and arbitration.

## Output
Fixture or clause review: deal facts, clause-by-clause risks with proposed wording, open points. Claim: laytime or off-hire statement, total, who pays whom, documents relied on (NOR, SOF, logs).

## Pitfalls
- Counting from an invalid NOR; misreading "time lost waiting for berth" wording.
- Applying exceptions after demurrage starts.
- No emissions clause: the ISM company (usually the owner) still surrenders allowances and pays FuelEU penalties; recovery from a time charterer then rests on national law (the ETS Directive gives a reimbursement right, verify locally), which is slower than a clause.
- Forgetting commissions and despatch in the final statement.
