## When to use
Assembling or auditing a technical file, drafting an EU or UK declaration, checking labels, working with a notified body, answering an authority's request.

## Technical file contents
Follow the annex of each act (LVD Annex III, EMC Annex II, RED Annex V; model in Decision 768/2008/EC Annex II). Guidance: Blue Guide 2022 (Commission Notice 2022/C 247/01).
1. Product description: models and variants, intended use, photos, software and hardware revision.
2. Design and manufacturing drawings, circuit diagrams, component and sub-assembly lists, with the explanations needed to read them.
3. Standards applied in full or in part with edition and date; for anything not covered, how each essential requirement is met.
4. Adequate risk analysis and assessment (required under every NLF act, EMC included: an EMC assessment of the apparatus).
5. Calculations and test reports: lab, accreditation, sample serial and revision, standard edition, results, deviations, uncertainty.
6. Critical components list with their certificates.
7. Copies of the DoC, labels, user instructions and safety information in the languages required.
8. Production controls that keep later units conforming.
Keep it 10 years after the last unit is placed on the market; supply it to an authority on reasoned request.

## EU Declaration of Conformity (768/2008 Annex III model)
1. Unique product identification (model, type, batch or serial).
2. Manufacturer name and address (and authorised representative).
3. Statement that the manufacturer alone is responsible for issuing it.
4. Object of the declaration, traceable, image optional.
5. Statement of conformity with each applicable Union act, listed by number.
6. Harmonised standards or other specifications, with dates.
7. Notified body name, number, task and certificate, where involved.
8. Additional information; place and date of issue; name, function and signature.
One DoC covers all applicable acts. Under RED a simplified DoC with a web address is allowed (Art. 10(9)).

## Markings and labels
- CE marking at least 5 mm high, visible, legible and indelible; notified body number after it when that body takes part in production control.
- Manufacturer name, trade name or mark, postal address; importer details; type, batch or serial; WEEE bin; battery marks.
- EU economic operator name and contact on product, packaging, parcel or accompanying document (Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 Art. 4).
- RED: usage restrictions per Member State on the packaging (Art. 10(10)).
- UK: UKCA or recognised CE, UK DoC citing designated standards, UK importer address.
- US: FCC Part 15 label text and SDoC compliance statement; Canada: ICES label.

## Notified bodies
Use one when the act requires it (e.g. RED without full harmonised standards, standards with OJ restrictions, machinery Annex I Part A). Confirm the body, act, module and product scope in NANDO; keep its certificates and the contract in the file.

## Market surveillance
Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 (since 16 July 2021) lets authorities request documents, sample, test, order corrective action, withdrawal or recall, and fine. Response: log the request and deadline, send the DoC and requested file extract, test reports for the shipped revision, and a corrective action plan if needed. Serious risks go to Safety Gate; consumer product accidents are notified under GPSR (EU) 2023/988.

## Pitfalls
Undated standards; a DoC signed by someone without authority; test reports for an older revision; missing translations or importer address; a file nobody can produce within the authority's deadline.
