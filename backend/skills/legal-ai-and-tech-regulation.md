## When to use
Classifying AI, scoping a digital product or financial ICT, regulatory roadmaps. Fix the role (provider, deployer, importer, distributor, manufacturer, financial entity, ICT provider), product, market and as-of date. As of Oct 2026.

## EU AI Act (Reg. (EU) 2024/1689, amended by the AI Omnibus, OJ L 2026/1744, in force 27 July 2026)
- Banned (Art 5): harmful manipulation; exploiting vulnerabilities; social scoring; crime prediction solely from profiling; untargeted face scraping; emotion recognition at work or school; biometric categorisation by sensitive traits; real-time remote biometric ID for police. Added (ba) non-consensual intimate imagery and (bb) CSAM generation, from 2 Dec 2026.
- High-risk: Annex I product-safety components; Annex III uses (biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, employment, credit and insurance scoring, benefits, policing, migration, justice). Art 6(3): Annex III systems doing only narrow or preparatory tasks that do not shape decisions are out, never if they profile people; document it.
- Transparency (Art 50): disclose AI interaction, label deepfakes, mark synthetic output.
- High-risk providers: risk management (Art 9), data governance (10), technical file (11), logging (12), instructions (13), human oversight (14), accuracy and cybersecurity (15), QMS (17), conformity assessment, CE mark, EU database, post-market monitoring, serious-incident reports. Deployers (Art 26): follow instructions, competent oversight, monitoring, logs 6+ months, inform workers and their representatives; FRIA (Art 27) for public bodies and services, credit and insurance.
- GPAI (Arts 53-55): technical file, downstream information, copyright policy, public training-data summary; systemic-risk models (presumed above 10^25 FLOP) add evaluations, adversarial testing, incident reports, security. The Code of Practice is a compliance route.
- Dates: bans and AI literacy (Art 4, now best-efforts measures) 2 Feb 2025; GPAI and penalties 2 Aug 2025 (earlier models: 2 Aug 2027); most rules 2 Aug 2026; Art 50(2) for generative systems already marketed: 2 Dec 2026; Annex III high-risk 2 Dec 2027; Annex I 2 Aug 2028.
- Fines up to EUR 35m or 7% (bans), 15m or 3% (most duties), 7.5m or 1% (false information); SMEs pay the lower figure.

## Other EU digital laws
| Law | Applies | Core duties |
|---|---|---|
| Data Act 2023/2854 | 12 Sep 2025; design duty for products placed from 12 Sep 2026 | user data access, fair B2B terms, cloud switching (fees end 12 Jan 2027) |
| NIS2 2022/2555 | 17 Oct 2024 (national laws); amendments proposed 20 Jan 2026 | Art 21 measures, Art 23 reports 24h / 72h / 1 month, board accountability (Art 20); fines EUR 10m or 2% (essential), 7m or 1.4% (important) |
| DORA 2022/2554 | 17 Jan 2025 | ICT risk framework; major-incident reports 4h after classification (max 24h) / 72h / 1 month; TLPT; ICT third-party register and contracts |
| CRA 2024/2847 | reporting 11 Sep 2026; main duties 11 Dec 2027 | secure by design, vulnerability handling, SBOM, 5+ year support period unless use is shorter; 24h / 72h reports on exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents; stricter conformity for class I, II and critical |

## Japan
AI Promotion Act (2025): principles, AI Strategy Headquarters, AI Basic Plan (Phase II adopted 14 July 2026), businesses' duty to cooperate, no fines. Soft law: AI Guidelines for Business v1.2 (31 Mar 2026). APPI governs personal data; Copyright Act Art 30-4 covers training use (verify locally).

## Output
Applicability matrix (law, role, in or out and why, deadline, duties, gap, owner); classification memo; dated roadmap.

## Pitfalls
Pre-omnibus dates; "AI" in the name taken as high-risk; missed deployer duties; guidance treated as law. Counsel confirms binding views.
