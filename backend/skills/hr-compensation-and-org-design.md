## When to use
Creating or fixing levels and bands, pricing jobs, budgeting merit, pay equity reviews and gap reports, restructures, spans and layers.

## Job architecture
- Families, sub-families, levels; parallel tracks: individual contributor (e.g. P1-P6), management (M1-M5), support (S1-S4); senior IC levels match director scope.
- Level descriptors on factors: impact, complexity, autonomy, knowledge, leadership, stakeholder reach. Evaluate by point-factor (score factors, sum, map to grade) or market slotting; record the rationale. Titles follow level.

## Bands and formulas
- Market pricing: match benchmark jobs to 2-3 surveys by content; age data to one date: rate x (1 + g)^(months/12), g = expected annual movement; choose a position (P50, or P75 for scarce skills).
- Midpoint = target market rate. Spread s = (max - min) / min; often 30-40% for support, 40-60% professional, 50-80% executive. Min = mid x 2/(2 + s); max = min x (1 + s).
- Midpoint progression between grades: often 8-20%, wider at senior grades; bands overlap.
- Compa-ratio = salary / band midpoint (92,000 / 100,000 = 0.92). Rough reading: 0.80-0.90 developing, 0.95-1.05 fully proficient, above 1.10 high in range.
- Range penetration = (salary - min) / (max - min).
- Merit matrix: increase % by rating x compa-ratio zone (lower in range gets more); budget = sum of salary x %.
- Red circle (above max: freeze or lump sum); green circle (below min: lift to min).

## Pay equity
1. Group work of equal value on objective criteria: skills, effort, responsibility, working conditions (Directive 2023/970).
2. Raw gap = (mean men - mean women) / mean men x 100; same with medians (UK, 250+ employers, snapshot 5 April private or 31 March public: also bonus gaps, share receiving bonus, pay quarters).
3. Adjusted gap: regress ln(pay) on legitimate factors (level, job, location, tenure, performance) plus a gender flag; gap about 100 x (e^b - 1)% where b is the gender coefficient.
4. Directive Art 10: a gap in average pay of 5% or more in any category of workers, not justified by objective gender-neutral criteria and not remedied within six months of the report, triggers a joint pay assessment with workers' representatives. Internally, also fix unexplained regression gaps; budget and retest.
- Directive dates: transposition 7 June 2026 (check national law); first reports by 7 June 2027 for 150+ workers (250+ yearly, 150-249 every 3 years), 100-149 from 2031; pay range before interview; no pay-history questions; answer pay information requests within 2 months.

## Org design
- Strategy and work first: capabilities, decision rights (RACI or RAPID), interfaces; then structure (functional, divisional, matrix, product or platform). Test options with Goold and Campbell's nine tests: fit (market advantage, parenting advantage, people, feasibility) and design (specialist cultures, difficult links, redundant hierarchy, accountability, flexibility).
- Span = direct reports per manager; layers = levels from CEO to front line. Wider spans fit standardised work, experienced teams and managers with little own work; narrow spans fit complex, novel or dispersed work. Flag managers with under 3 reports and unexplained layers.
- Restructures: consultation duties (EU 98/59/EC; UK 20+ redundancies at one site in 90 days: 30 days, 100+: 45 days, verify ERA 2025 changes; works councils), objective selection criteria, equality impact check.

## Output
Level guide; band table (grade, min, mid, max, spread); compa-ratio distribution; equity analysis with method, gaps and remediation cost; structure options with span and layer metrics and trade-offs.

## Pitfalls
One survey; no location differentials; regressing on tainted factors (starting pay carries history); restructures that only add managers.
