## When to use
Assessing a port or terminal, planning flows around congestion, choosing equipment, explaining delays or rolled cargo, or setting up reefer shipments.

## Containers
| Type (ISO 6346 code) | External L x W x H (m) | Volume | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 ft dry (22G1) | 6.058 x 2.438 x 2.591 | ~33 m3 | dense cargo |
| 40 ft dry (42G1) | 12.192 x 2.438 x 2.591 | ~67 m3 | general |
| 40 ft high cube (45G1) | height 2.896 | ~76 m3 | light, bulky |
| 40 ft reefer HC (45R1) | as HC | ~67 m3 inside | chilled, frozen |
| open top, flat rack, tank, ventilated | varies | varies | out-of-gauge, liquids |
- ISO maximum gross mass 36,000 kg for standard sizes; the CSC plate shows ratings and stacking strength; road limits are usually lower.
- Number: 4-letter BIC owner code ending in U, 6 digits, check digit.
- Reefers: set point, chilled or frozen, fresh-air vent (m3/h), humidity, controlled atmosphere; pre-trip inspection; load cargo pre-cooled (a reefer holds temperature, it does not pull down warm cargo); plug-in and monitoring at the terminal.
- Packing to the IMO/ILO/UNECE CTU Code; even weight, secured, VGM declared under SOLAS VI/2.

## Terminal metrics
| Metric | Definition | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Gross crane rate | moves per crane per working hour | 25-35 typical for modern quay cranes |
| Berth productivity | moves per hour at berth, all cranes | drives port stay |
| Yard utilisation | occupied slots / capacity | above about 80 percent, productivity drops fast |
| Dwell time | days per box (import, export, transshipment) | rising dwell signals congestion |
| Truck turn time | gate in to gate out | appointment systems cut it |
| Berth waiting time | arrival at anchorage to all fast | queue length |
- Berth window: weekly slot agreed by carrier and terminal; off-window arrivals wait or bump others.
- Cut-offs: booking, shipping instructions, VGM, CY gate-in; a miss rolls the box.

## Network and reliability
- Hub-and-spoke with transshipment (Singapore, Tanjung Pelepas, Colombo, Algeciras) vs direct calls; each transshipment adds dwell and missed-connection risk.
- Alliances from February 2025: Gemini Cooperation (Maersk, Hapag-Lloyd), Premier Alliance (ONE, HMM, Yang Ming), Ocean Alliance (CMA CGM, COSCO and OOCL, Evergreen); MSC alone (verify current line-up).
- Blank (void) sailings cancel a weekly departure to cut capacity or recover schedules; expect rolled cargo and pressure on the next sailing.
- Schedule reliability = share of arrivals on time within a defined window. August 2026: global 49.9 percent, the lowest since September 2022; late vessels averaged 6.81 days; cause was severe Asian port congestion; Maersk 67.3 and Hapag-Lloyd 61.5 percent led (Sea-Intelligence).
- SOLAS amendments in force 1 January 2026 require masters to report containers lost or seen drifting.

## Diagnosing congestion
Check in order: vessels waiting and waiting days, yard utilisation, dwell by flow, gate turn time, empty-box stock, chassis and labour, bunched arrivals after blank sailings. Mitigate with alternative ports, earlier cut-offs, off-dock storage or a later booked sailing.

## Pitfalls
- Booking the last sailing before a cut-off at a congested port.
- Reading one month of reliability as a trend.
- A box legal at sea but overweight for the road.
